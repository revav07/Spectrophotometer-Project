# Spectrophotometer Project Log 

## Day 0 - Preparation and Initial Wiring
### What I Did 
- Researched the basic operating principles of spectrophotometry and selected the necessary electronic components
- Created a preliminary wiring diagram for the prototype circuit
- Programmed the Arduino to control individual LED colors and record corresponding photoresistor readings
- Validated system functionality through iterative testing and troubleshooting

### Challenges
- Researching commercial spectrophotometer designs and identifying the minimum set of components needed to create a functional prototype
- Translating professional laboratory instrumentation into a simplified, low-cost system while maintaining the core principles of absorbance measurement
- Debugging hardware issues, including short circuits caused by accidental contact between RGB LED pins
